| **Primary Actor** | Customer |
| **Secondary Actors** | Payment Gateway, SMS Service, Personal Trainer |
| **Trigger** | Customer opens an Approved Personal Trainer profile and selects **Book a PT Package**. |
| **Description** | Customer books a PT package, completes online payment, and creates a PT booking with status **Pending** waiting for PT response (UC-12) within 24 hours (BR-08). |
| **Preconditions** | 1. Customer is authenticated. 2. Customer has an **ACTIVE** membership with at least one valid day remaining (BR-06). 3. Selected Personal Trainer account is **Approved** (BR-07). 4. Selected PT package is active and available. |
| **Post conditions** | 1. On success: PT booking is created with status `Pending`; payment SUCCESS is recorded; booking request is sent to the PT; Customer receives in-app/SMS notification. 2. On failure: no Confirmed booking is created; failed/uncertain payments are recorded for reconciliation. |
| **Normal Flow** | 1. Customer opens an Approved PT profile. 2. Customer selects an available PT package. 3. System displays package name, sessions, price, validity, and scheduling info. 4. Customer selects preferred date/time and optionally enters a note. 5. Customer confirms booking details. 6. System validates membership, PT account, package, time, and price. 7. System creates a pending payment request and sends it to Payment Gateway («include» Process Payment). 8. Payment Gateway returns SUCCESS. 9. System records payment and creates PT booking with status `Pending`. 10. System notifies the Personal Trainer. 11. System sends in-app/SMS confirmation to Customer and shows booking reference. |
| **Alternative Flows** | 1. **Apply promotion code**: At step 4, Customer enters a promo code; System validates and recalculates amount; continue at step 6. 2. **Payment declined**: At step 8, Gateway returns Fail; System records failed payment, does not notify PT for booking, allows retry/cancel. 3. **Cancel before payment**: Before step 8, Customer cancels; System cancels pending payment request; no valid booking is created. |
| **Exceptions** | 1. **Gateway timeout / uncertain response**: At step 8, no timely response; System marks payment Pending/Unknown for reconciliation; does not treat booking as paid/Confirmed; informs Customer. 2. **Package or schedule unavailable**: At step 6, package inactive or time invalid; System rejects and shows reason; Customer must reselect. |
| **Priority** | High |
| **Frequency of Use** | High during peak booking periods. |
| **Business Rules** | BR-06: ACTIVE membership required to book PT. BR-08: PT must respond to Pending within 24 hours (handled in UC-12 / SMD-02). Confirm/Reject is UC-12 — not «extend». |
